## Identify the unit before reading a count

| Term | Meaning and use |
| --- | --- |
| `code_tract`, `id_2010`, `id_2022` | Original census tract identifiers. Preserve their text representation; never average IDs. |
| `common_id` | A connected comparison group linking tract membership across years. Its number does not imply geographic order. |
| `part_id` | Unique reference geometry piece identifier; repeated tract IDs can have several pieces. |
| `duplicate_base_ids` | Number of repeated-ID reference rows beyond the first occurrence of each ID, not the number of distinct affected IDs. |
| `n_2010`, `n_2022` | Distinct tract membership counts in a common group. Some income tables use separate `n_*` diagnostics for income aggregation: read the income section below. |
| `relationship` | `1:1` (one-to-one), `1:n` (split), `n:1` (merge), or `many_to_many`; based on yearly group membership. |
| Source record | One input row, possibly sharing a project ID with another row. |
| Project ID | Provider identifier; distinct IDs and source rows are different counts. |
| Geometry component | A decomposed point, line or polygon part. One record may contribute many components. |
| `area_km2` | Saved area of the merged common geometry, measured in equal-area EPSG:6933; density denominator. |

The active analytical geography is one merged 2022 feature per common_id. Its yearly footprints are not guaranteed identical; the researcher accepts the documented inhabited-population assumption. Reference pieces stay intact. Dissolving retains holes and source gaps; it does not fill them, and narrow holes can look like interior strokes at display scale. Municipality selection retains complete connected groups. [Harmonisation report and diagrams](../research/reports/ibge_census_tract_harmonisation.md).

`footprint_difference_fraction` is the area of the symmetric difference of yearly merged footprints divided by their union area: 0 means identical footprints, 0.10 means 10% of the combined footprint belongs to only one year. It is a geographic diagnostic, not an estimate of affected residents or an exclusion rule. `area_2010_km2` and `area_2022_projected_km2` use EPSG:5880 for that diagnostic; they differ from the EPSG:6933 `area_km2` density denominator.

## Census proportions: source codes and denominators

Prefixes identify the imported census tables; codes are year-specific. For each row below, sum the numerator and denominator within a common area, then divide. Never average tract ratios. The implemented household denominator is `domicilio01_V002` in 2010 and `domicilio01_V00001` in 2022; consult provider definitions for each year's universe rather than assuming universes are identical.

| Indicator/output | 2010 numerator | 2022 numerator | Denominator |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Black / `black` | `pessoa03_V003` | `raca_V01318` | 2010 `pessoa03_V001`; 2022 Basico `V0001` |
| Brown / `brown` | `pessoa03_V005` | `raca_V01320` | Same total-population denominator |
| Black + Brown / `black_brown` | Sum of the two codes above | Sum of the two codes above | Same total-population denominator |
| Literacy aged 15+ / `literacy` | Sum `pessoa01_V012`–`V077` | Sum `alfabetizacao_V00748`–`V00760` | 2010 sum `pessoa13_V049`–`V134`; 2022 sum `alfabetizacao_V00644`–`V00656` |
| Rudimentary sanitation / `rudimentary_sanitation` | `domicilio01_V019` + `V020` + `V021` | `domicilio02_V00312` + `V00313` + `V00314` | Household denominator above |
| Water network / `water_network` | `domicilio01_V012` | `domicilio02_V00111` | Household denominator above |
| Waste collection / `waste_collection` | `domicilio01_V035` | `domicilio02_V00397` + `V00398` | Household denominator above |
| Sewer/septic / `sewer_septic` | `domicilio01_V017` + `V018` | `domicilio02_V00309` + `V00310` + `V00311` | Household denominator above |
| Houses / `houses` | `domicilio01_V003` | `domicilio01_V00047` | Household denominator above |
| Village/condominium houses / `village_condo` | `domicilio01_V004` | `domicilio01_V00048` | Household denominator above |
| Apartments / `apartments` | `domicilio01_V005` | `domicilio01_V00049` | Household denominator above |

Annual `*_ratio_2010` and `*_ratio_2022` fields are fractions: 0.10 means 10%. `*_ratio_change` is 2022 minus 2010 in fractions. `*_ratio_change_pp` multiplies that difference by 100: 10% → 15% means +5 percentage points. This differs from relative percentage growth.

**Missingness:** outer joins preserve a missing year. Ratios require positive denominators. Specified combined-count components are coalesced to zero; most grouped count sums skip missing values and can become zero if all entries are missing. Housing denominators retain missingness when all contributors are missing. Missing-component treatment can bias available ratios; an available estimate does not prove complete coverage. Literacy uses the 2022 Pessoas denominator independently of the Basico table. Details and calculation exceptions are in [methods](methods.md).

## Income and inflation

| Variable | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 2010 `V020`, `V021`, `V022` | Household heads, income recipients and total income; imported columns are identified by their code suffix. |
| 2022 `V06001`, `V06004` | Household heads and tract mean income. |
| Tract mean, 2010 | `V022 / V021`, requiring finite positive recipients and finite nonnegative total income. |
| Group mean | Sum of tract mean × household heads / sum of usable household heads. Both sums use the same pairs: positive finite weights and finite nonnegative means. |
| `income_2010_nominal` | Original nominal 2010 group mean. |
| `income_2010`, `income_2022` | Comparable means in July 2022 BRL; 2010 is multiplied by configured inflation_2022 / inflation_2010 (default 6645.74 / 3200.06). |
| `income_change_brl` | 2022 minus adjusted 2010 income, in BRL. |
| `income_change_pct` | 100 × (2022 / adjusted 2010 − 1), requiring positive 2010 income. |
| `n_used_*`, `heads_used_*`, `tract_coverage_pct_*` | Usable tract pairs, their head-count weights, and usable pairs as a percentage of contributing tracts. |
| `missing_weights_*`, `missing_means_*`, `complete_*` | Diagnostics for unavailable/invalid weights and means. Incomplete coverage does not automatically reject the group mean. |

The former report claimed stronger restrictions on multi-tract means than the original code imposed. Current explanations describe the preserved code; no weighting rule was changed. In final joined income tables `n_2010`/`n_2022` are tract-membership metadata; additional merge-suffixed `n_*` fields originate from the income aggregation. Inspect the table schema before using them. An unweighted median across common-area incomes is not the mean income of Brazil's population.

## Population density

`pop_2010` and `pop_2022` are aggregated population counts, with all-missing totals preserved. `population_density_<year> = pop_<year> / area_km2`, requiring positive area. Absolute change is people/km²; `population_density_change_pct` is relative growth from a positive 2010 density. The archived stored absolute-change column repeated percentage changes; the current implementation follows the source calculation. See [validation results](checks.md) for the baseline discrepancy.

## Conference sanitation charts

These charts use a broader inadequate-sanitation definition than temporal rudimentary sanitation: add 2010 `domicilio01_V023`, and 2022 `domicilio02_V00307` + `V00308`, to the respective rudimentary-sanitation components. Any missing/invalid required component excludes the tract from both count sums. Every contributing 2022 tract must be urban (`situacao = Urbana`).

`racial_quintile` divides eligible areas into five groups ordered by their 2022 Black/Brown share, then common_id to break ties; larger buckets come first. The change chart uses paired years and household-weighted percentages. The income chart has a separate eligible 2022 population, uses income tertiles calculated before selecting Q1/Q5, and right-closed intervals. Its boxes summarise area-level sanitation percentages; hidden outliers are retained. [Current charts and tables](../research/reports/brazil_poroject_charts.md).

## Exploratory boundary and race outputs

The 2000 overview uses spherical R sf/S2 overlap fractions of newer tracts. In `change_summary_2000_*`, `n` counts intersections; `over5pct` counts qualifying overlaps; `minimum`, `maximum` and `total` summarise overlap fractions; `change_type` records the original stable/split/reshaped/unmatched rule. A fraction above 0.05 qualifies; one qualifying match above 0.95 is stable. This is separate from largest-overlap connected-group harmonisation.

The original race exploration uses raw annual tracts across six municipalities. `black_pop / pop` gives `black_ratio`; map bands are presentation categories. Nonfinite ratios are omitted from visualisation while stored analytical values retain original semantics. Boxplots weight mapped geometry observations equally, including any repeated tract IDs in the preserved source geometry. They are not population-weighted city estimates, and they do not deduplicate those identifiers. [Exploration report](../research/reports/ibge_census_data_extraction.md).

## Infrastructure variables and exclusions

| Field/result | Definition and limitation |
| --- | --- |
| Obrasgov `tipo`, `subtipo` → `type_en`, `subtype_en` | Supplied categories and configured translations. Unmapped values stay missing. |
| IBAMA `tp_setor` → `tp_sector_en` | Supplied sector and English translation; alternate `tp_setor_en` spelling is retained for compatibility. |
| Frequency-table `projects` | Source-record count, despite the legacy column name. Repeated project IDs mean this is not necessarily distinct projects. |
| Frequency-table `share` | Fraction of all classified source records, computed before keeping the largest 15 categories. Missing classifications are excluded. |
| Original cleaning `removed` | Source rows excluded for empty or world-range-invalid geometry; retained geometries are repaired. It is not an offshore/state exclusion count. |
| `count_matches_baseline` | Retained-row count equals the archived cleaned count; it does not prove record, geometry or attribute parity. |
| `location_status`, map eligibility | Component-level screening, quarantining suspicious/invalid locations while retaining uncertain coastal/border/marine cases for review. Eligibility is not certified geocoding. |
| `uf_principal`, primary-state check | Declared primary state versus complete component geometry: fully covered, partly intersecting/boundary contact, entirely outside, unassessable or unknown state. No buffering or coordinate correction. |
| State-filter exclusions | Approved removal of entirely outside components from the derivative. Previously quarantined parts are not counted again as newly removed map parts. Partial intersections retain baseline eligibility. |
| Investments | Source-row sums and distinct-ID consistent-budget diagnostics are different measures. Geometry decomposition must not multiply budgets. |

All Obrasgov polygons are excluded from map display at researcher request. Original geometry, project records and budgets remain preserved. A point/line eligible derivative is not a corrected source dataset. IBGE territory is not an offshore licensing boundary; Natural Earth references are generalized and may omit small islands. The [preprocessing report](../research/reports/obrasgov_preprocessing.md) shows current screening and exclusions with figures. Source totals, components and distinct IDs must never be compared as if they used the same denominator.

Annex I includes `zona_homogenea`, `zona_uso`, `valor_m2_terreno` and abbreviated valuation columns (`ap`, `ba`, `ca`, `lj`, `vr`, `gp`, `sl`, `vc`); consult the original legal reference for definitions rather than inferring expansions. These are exploratory imported reference values, not authenticated market observations. The exact cadastral CSV is missing; processing cannot complete. The legacy stage metadata’s income `missing_change` counter was recorded before final income-change columns existed. Report availability is instead counted from final saved columns; the original counter remains labelled legacy provenance.

Generated timestamps describe processing, not observation dates. Hashes identify exact files and code; they are not scientific confidence scores. Reports expose complete records in collapsed technical disclosures, while `docs/evidence/` and `outputs/reports/evidence/` retain preservation and execution evidence. Report cells read current artifacts; acquisition occurs only through explicit acquisition commands. The diagrams illustrate a method unless a caption explicitly identifies actual source geometry.
